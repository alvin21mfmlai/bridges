How to read a curve

Historical fit (left panel)

Each inspection interval starts at its observed condition. Dashed segments are one-step fitted predictions, and the vertical gaps to the observations are fitting errors.

Forecast (right panel)

The orange curve continues from the latest inspection for 20 years. It assumes no maintenance or intervention.

Poor/severe share

The percentage of the element's quantity in condition states 3 and 4. The rate is the element-ID deterioration rate per year.

Fitting RMSE

Error of the fit in percentage points, shown next to a persistence baseline. It is a fitting measure, not out-of-sample validation.
