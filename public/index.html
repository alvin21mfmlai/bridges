Age band summary

Reference

How the profiles are computed

Data

National Bridge Inventory element condition records by state, element type and data year, matched to NBI inventory items for year built and deck area.

Coverage is listed on the map in Step 1.

Period

A period is 5 consecutive data years. A state with 5 or fewer data years uses all of them.

Each bridge counts once per element type: at its latest record within the period.

Bins

  • Age: bridge age in the record's data year (data year − year built), in 5-year bands 1–5 … 96–100.
  • CS%: the element quantity in a condition state ÷ its total quantity × 100, in 5% bands [0,5], (5,10] … (95,100].
  • CS1–CS4: Good, Fair, Poor and Severe condition states; each has its own figure.

Bar values

  • Deck area: the total deck area (sq ft) of the bridges in the cell.
  • % of total: the cell's deck area ÷ the deck area of all bridges in the profile. Each figure sums to 100%.
  • % within age band: the cell's deck area ÷ the deck area of its age band. Each age band sums to 100% in each figure.

Exclusions

Records with no element quantity, a failed quantity check, no deck area, no year built, or an age outside 1–100 years are left out and counted under the profile.

All reported figures are rounded to 3 significant figures.
