Data

National Bridge Inventory element condition records by state, element type and data year, matched to NBI inventory items for year built and deck area.

Coverage is listed on the map in Step 1.

Period

A period runs from any start year to any end year, as long as it holds at least five years of data. A state with five or fewer years of data uses all of them. Each bridge still counts once, at its latest inspection inside the period, so a longer period adds bridges that were not inspected recently but does not count any bridge twice.

Each bridge is counted once per element type, using its most recent inspection in the period.

Bins

  • Age: bridge age in the record's data year (data year − year built), in 5-year bands 1–5 … 96–100.
  • CS%: the element quantity in a condition state ÷ its total quantity × 100, in 5% bands [0,5], (5,10] … (95,100].
  • CS1–CS4: the Good, Fair, Poor and Severe condition states. Each has its own chart.

Bar values

  • Deck area: the total deck area (sq ft) of the bridges in the cell.
  • % of total: the cell's deck area ÷ the deck area of all bridges in the profile. Each chart adds up to 100%.
  • % within age band: the cell's deck area ÷ the deck area of its age band. Each age band adds up to 100%.

Exclusions

Records with no element quantity, a failed quantity check, no deck area, no year built, or an age outside 1–100 years are left out. The number left out is shown with each profile.

All reported figures are rounded to 3 significant figures.
