How the curves are generated

Model

b(t) = 1 − (1 − b₀) · e−r·t
  • b: The share of the element's quantity in poor or severe condition (condition states 3 and 4).
  • b₀: The share at the start.
  • t: Time in years.
  • r: The element-ID deterioration rate per year.

Intervals

Consecutive inspections used in the fit form an interval.

An interval is excluded when the element's total quantity changes between its two inspections.

Each remaining interval gets an equal weight.

Fitting

r ≥ 0 minimizes the weighted squared error between each interval's predicted and observed end share.

Fitting RMSE is reported in percentage points next to a persistence baseline (no change).

This is a fitting measure, not out-of-sample validation.

Forecast

The curve starts at the latest inspection and runs for the selected horizon, assuming no intervention.

All reported figures are rounded to 3 significant figures.
